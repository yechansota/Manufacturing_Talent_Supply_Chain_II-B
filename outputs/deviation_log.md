Any change to `config/prereg.yaml` after it is frozen (see `outputs/freeze_record.md`)
must be recorded here BEFORE the affected analysis is run.
Changes made before the freeze are recorded in `prereg.yaml -> meta.changelog`, not here.

Types: **clarification** = an implementation reading of frozen text that the text leaves open
(no change to prereg.yaml); **addition** = an extra sensitivity analysis that does not replace
any pre-registered primary; **change** = a modification of frozen text.
